About Brew Haven

Built by coffee lovers, for coffee lovers

Who we are

Brew Haven is a fictional local coffee shop that provides freshly brewed coffee, tea, pastries and light meals to students, professionals and members of the local community. The business aims to create a comfortable environment where customers can enjoy quality beverages and food. Its mission is to provide affordable, high-quality coffee and excellent customer service. Its vision is to become a popular and trusted coffee destination within the local community. The primary target audience includes students, young professionals, coffee enthusiasts and people looking for a comfortable place to meet or work.

Located near the university campus, we designed Brew Haven to be more than just a place to grab coffee. We wanted to create a community hub where ideas are exchanged and friendships are formed.

Mission, vision and audience

Our mission

The main goal of the website is to establish an online presence for Brew Haven and make information about the business easily accessible. The website should increase customer awareness, display the menu and encourage customers to visit the coffee shop. Key performance indicators will include website visits, menu views, contact enquiries and customer engagement.

Our vision

Our vision is to become the most popular and trusted coffee destination within the local community, inspiring positive change and bringing people together.

Our target audience

We primarily target students, young professionals, remote workers, coffee enthusiasts, and community members seeking a relaxed atmosphere.
